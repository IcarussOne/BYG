# Structures

All of these can be turned off together with the `structuresContentEnabled` setting, and (except the Dead Sea shipwreck and Enchanted Village) each is also tied to its wood set's toggle. Chances below are the base chance per chunk, before the `templateChanceMultiplier` setting scales them.

## Villages

| Village | Biome | Chance / million |
|---|---|:-:|
| Bayou Village | Bayou | 3200 |
| Deciduous Village | Deciduous Forest | 3200 |
| Deciduous Village (Seasonal) | Seasonal Deciduous | 3200 |
| Lake Village | Great Lakes | 3200 |
| Orchard Village | Orchard | 3200 |
| Quagmire Village | Quagmire | 3200 |
| Red Village | Red Desert | 3200 |
| Salem Village | Whispering Woods | 9500 |

Bayou, Lake and Quagmire villages sit over the water in their biome. The others sit on grass.

## Other buildings

| Structure | Biome | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Bayou Witch Hut | Bayou | Chance 3500/million. |
| Cypress Tower | Cypress Swamplands | Chance 4000/million, placed over water. |
| Enchanted Village | Enchanted Forest | About a 0.3% chance per chunk. Can appear underground as well as on the surface. Needs the glowcane wood set enabled. |

## Dead Sea Shipwreck

Found only in the **Dead Sea**. Each wreck is one of three styles picked at random - a small sloop (40% chance), a larger cog (30%), or a wreck crashed into a rock spire (30%) - and only appears where the sea floor is level enough for the whole hull.

- The main hull may contain a **Chest**.
- The shipwreck may contain a **Crate**.
