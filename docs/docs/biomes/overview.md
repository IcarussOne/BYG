# Biomes

BYG adds 76 biomes. They are grouped on these pages by environment:

- [Forests](forests.md): Woodland and forest biomes.
- [Taiga and Snow](taiga-and-snow.md): Taiga, snowy and glacial biomes.
- [Wetlands](wetlands.md): Swamps, bayous, marshes and lakes.
- [Deserts and Canyons](deserts-and-canyons.md): Hot, dry and canyon biomes.
- [Grasslands and Savanna](grasslands-and-savanna.md): Plains, prairies, meadows and savannas.
- [Mountains and Highlands](mountains-and-highlands.md): Mountain and high-altitude biomes.
- [Tropical and Islands](tropical-and-islands.md): Jungle, tropical and island biomes.
- [Ocean Biomes](ocean-biomes.md): Ocean biomes, including the Dead Sea.
