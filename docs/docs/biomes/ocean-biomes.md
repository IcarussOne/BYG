Ocean biomes.

## Dead Sea {#byg_dead_sea}

<BiomeScreenshot id="byg_dead_sea" name="Dead Sea" />

The Dead Sea is a hot ocean with dark water and a floor of black sand. Its sparse seafloor has rocky arches, stone pillars and spikes, caves, scattered bones, and occasional [shipwrecks](../structures.md#dead-sea-shipwreck). Guardians are the biome's listed spawn.
