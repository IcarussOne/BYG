Woodland and forest biomes.

## Ancient Forest {#byg_ancient_forest}

<BiomeScreenshot id="byg_ancient_forest" name="Ancient Forest" />

The Ancient Forest is a rare, dense woodland with towering great oaks and an understory of smaller trees. Ferns, leaf litter, mushrooms, brambles, and occasional webs cover its shaded floor. Very rarely, a massive great oak rises above the canopy.
