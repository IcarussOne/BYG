# Creatures

BYG adds three creatures. Each has its own content toggle and, for the natural ones, a spawn-weight setting; see [Creature Settings](../getting-started/configuration.md#creature-settings) and the [Content Settings](../getting-started/configuration.md#content-settings). Turning a creature off unregisters it entirely: no spawn egg, and any that already exist in a loaded world disappear.

- [Kiwi Bird](kiwi-bird.md): A passive forager found in temperate forest biomes.
- [Crystal Crawler](crystal-crawler.md): A hostile spider found in the Crystal Canyons.
- [Fungal Zombie](fungal-zombie.md): A hostile zombie variant found in the Fungal Jungle.
