# Brewing

If Baobab content and the Clarity brewing option are both on, you can brew the **Potion of Clarity**. It uses the normal brewing stand:

| Step | Base | Ingredient | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Water bottle | Baobab Powder | Potion of Clarity |
| 2 | Potion of Clarity | Gunpowder | Splash Potion of Clarity |
| 3 | Splash Potion of Clarity | Dragon's Breath | Lingering Potion of Clarity |

The potion lasts 300 ticks (15 seconds). While Clarity is active you **cannot receive any other potion effect**, and drinking it removes all effects you already have. Baobab Powder is crafted from Baobab Fruit (1 fruit gives 1 powder).

The option is `clarityPotionBrewingEnabled` in the [item settings](getting-started/configuration.md#item-settings).
